MP4, WebM, MOV — stays on your device, nothing is uploaded anywhere
YouTube / TikTok / IG links won't load here — pulling video off those platforms needs a server on their end (that's how Opus Clip does it too) and no browser-only tool can do it without violating their Terms of Service. If it's your own video, use YouTube Studio's "Download" option (or yt-dlp if you have rights to the clip) to get the file, then drop it in above. Uploaded files also export more reliably than remote URLs — see the export note below.
Export keeps your source resolution (4K in → 4K out) with bitrate scaled up accordingly, and saves as MP4 where your browser supports it (Chrome, Edge, Safari), falling back to WebM otherwise. It renders in real time — a 4K clip on a slower device may take a little longer than its own runtime to export. If "Live-translate" is on, it'll keep translating just ahead of the export too — for a smooth burn, run "Translate all" first on anything you plan to export, so nothing's left waiting on a network call mid-render.
