YouTube / TikTok / IG links won't load here — pulling video off those platforms needs a server on their end (that's how Opus Clip does it too) and no browser-only tool can do it without violating their Terms of Service. If it's your own video, use YouTube Studio's "Download" option (or yt-dlp if you have rights to the clip) to get the file, then drop it in above. Uploaded files also export more reliably than remote URLs — see the export note below.
Load a video first.
Pick the export quality in the dropdown — it lists your video's original size plus any smaller sizes you can scale down to (never larger, so a 1080p video can't be upscaled). Bitrate scales with the size you choose, and it saves as MP4 where your browser supports it (Chrome, Edge, Safari), falling back to WebM otherwise. It renders in real time — a 4K clip on a slower device may take a little longer than its own runtime to export. If "Live-translate" is on, it'll keep translating just ahead of the export too — for a smooth burn, run "Translate all" first on anything you plan to export, so nothing's left waiting on a network call mid-render.
